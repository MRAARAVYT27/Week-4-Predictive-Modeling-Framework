# References and Data Sources

The following resources can support the development of the proposed agricultural predictive modeling framework. The dataset ultimately used for experiments will be identified separately after its suitability and available variables have been checked.

1. **FAOSTAT — Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations**  
   Agricultural production and related statistical data.  
   https://www.fao.org/faostat/

2. **Government of India, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare**  
   Agricultural statistics and publications relevant to Indian agriculture.  
   https://agriwelfare.gov.in/

3. **Pandas Documentation**  
   https://pandas.pydata.org/docs/

4. **Scikit-learn Documentation**  
   https://scikit-learn.org/stable/

5. **Matplotlib Documentation**  
   https://matplotlib.org/stable/

6. **Seaborn Documentation**  
   https://seaborn.pydata.org/

7. **NumPy Documentation**  
   https://numpy.org/doc/

## Notes

The resources listed above are potential references, not confirmation that a specific dataset has already been downloaded or analyzed. The final report should identify the exact dataset, its source, relevant columns, and any data limitations once the dataset has been selected.

All reported model metrics and analytical findings must come from experiments actually performed on the selected dataset.
